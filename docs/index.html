A punch clock that knows nothing about you.

One button, «Punch in/out», and a list of your times. Every punch is encrypted on your phone. The server never sees a working time. No e-mail address, no real name.

Open Zeropunch Source on GitHub

Runs in the browser · installable · works offline · punch with an NFC sticker · English and German · AGPL-3.0

One button. That is all.

There is only one kind of stamp: a point in time. What it means follows from the order.

Punch with an NFC sticker

Stick an NFC sticker on your locker, desk, notebook or bag and hold your phone to it. The punch is made. On an iPhone your work badge often does the job too, if it has an NFC chip. Nothing on the badge is changed. The sticker holds nothing about you. The instructions are in the settings.

Badge reminder

Does your badge need validating every so often? The reminder comes up every 25 days with the first punch of the day. You choose the number of days.

One button: «Punch in/out»

Tap it when you start. Tap it when you stop. The start screen shows whether you are in or out, and today’s total. Swipe sideways for earlier days. Four symbols at the bottom lead to the list, nerd mode and the settings.

Punches pair up in order

The 1st punch of the day is in, the 2nd out, the 3rd in again: 08:02 – 12:01, 12:48 – 17:10. Change one, and the day pairs up anew.

Day and week totals

The list shows every day with its total, the newest on top, and every week against your target. Every punch can be changed or deleted. Forgot one? «+» adds a punch or an absence. The filter button shows the holidays ahead and finds days with a break too short, a punch missing or less than 11 hours of rest.

Break rules by country

Did you take the break the law asks for? Choose the rule of Switzerland and Liechtenstein, Germany or Austria, or set your own. By default it follows your holiday calendar.

Night shifts, if you need them

Move the change of day, for example to 04:00. A shift over midnight stays one day.

Absences and a sheet to print

Vacation, sick, time off in lieu or a public holiday: mark a whole or half day, or a range of days. Vacation and sick days count towards your target. Time off in lieu counts nothing: it uses up your overtime. In nerd mode there is a sheet for every week and month to print or save as a PDF.

Public holidays by themselves

Choose your canton or country, and its public holidays come by themselves. A holiday lowers your target for the day. The list shows only those from Monday to Friday.

Nerd mode

Balance for the week, the month and 12 months. Hours per week and per month. For a period you choose: typical times as medians, records, absences and heatmaps of presence, breaks, punches and balance. Your IP address only if you ask. Bring old times along from a CSV file, read on your phone only.

Your size, your colour

Text size, light, dark or automatic, eight colours and the wording of the button: «Punch in/out» or «Clock in/out». The settings come in groups, with jump buttons on top.

Offline, and like an app

Add Zeropunch to your home screen. Without a connection you keep punching. The punches go out when you are online again.

What the server knows

Your browser encrypts every punch before it is sent (AES-256-GCM). The server keeps only what it needs to log you in and to sync. It can read none of it.

The server seesThe server never sees

  • The server seesan HMAC of your username, the name itself only while you register or log in

    The server never seesyour username at rest

  • The server seesHMACs of the login keys, and your master key, wrapped

    The server never seesyour password, the unwrapped master key

  • The server seesfixed-size ciphertext per punch

    The server never seestimes, notes, settings

  • The server seeswhen an encrypted write arrives. It stores only the day, never a time of day.

    The server never seeswhat it contains

  • The server seesyour IP address as a keyed hash in rate-limit counters, for two days at most

Honest limits

  • You must trust the JavaScript the server delivers. As with every web app that encrypts in the browser: a compromised server could ship code that leaks your password. What helps: no third-party code, a strict Content Security Policy, no build step, public source.
  • Arrival times are visible. Punching live sends a record at that moment. The operator could guess your times from that. A punch made offline goes out later.
  • A lost password cannot be recovered. No back door, no reset by e-mail. The punches stay unreadable, and the account is deleted after 61 days without use.
  • Usernames are not secret. Registering tells you whether a name is taken. Pick one that does not identify you.
  • A long password matters. Whoever obtained the database and the server secret could try passwords offline, at 600 000 PBKDF2 rounds per guess.

No e-mail, no real name

An account is two things. Only you know the second one.

  1. A username

    3–32 characters, a–z 0–9 . _ -. No real name needed, better none.

  2. A password

    At least 6 characters, better 10 or more, best several words. It never leaves your device. Changing it logs out your other devices.

  3. No reset

    Nobody can reset your password, not even us. Without it the punches can no longer be read. Pick one you will remember.

Limits, stated plainly

Zeropunch keeps as little as it can, for as short as it can.

13months per punch

After 13 months a punch is deleted for good, on your device and on the server. Export first what you want to keep: CSV or JSON.

12punches a day

The app takes no more than 12 punches a day. The button then waits for the next day.

61days without use

An account that is not used for 61 days is deleted with all its punches. The app warns you before that.

Free and open source

Zeropunch is free software under the AGPL-3.0. No trackers, no cookies except the login session. You can also run your own copy. The README explains every step.

Self-hosting guide

Questions

Can anyone see my working times?

Not on the server. Every punch is encrypted in your browser. The server only sees when an encrypted write arrives.

What if I forget my password?

Then nobody can read your punches any more. A password cannot be reset. The account is deleted after 61 days without use. While you are still logged in, you can change the password in the settings.

I punched wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes, in the list. Every punch has a pencil to change it and a bin to delete it. Add a forgotten punch with «+», up to 13 months back.

Do I have to install an app?

No. Zeropunch runs in the browser. You can add it to your home screen, and it keeps punching without a connection.

How long are my punches kept?

13 months, then they are deleted for good. Export first what you want to keep, as CSV or JSON.

Can I punch with an NFC sticker?

Yes. Stick an NFC sticker on your locker, desk, notebook or bag and hold your phone to it. On an iPhone your work badge often does the job too, if it has an NFC chip. The instructions are in the settings.

Can I record holidays and sick days and print my times?

Yes. Absences are entered as a whole or half day, or from–until. Vacation and sick days count towards your target. Public holidays lower it, and those of your canton or country come by themselves. Time off in lieu counts nothing: it uses up overtime. In nerd mode there is a sheet for every week and month to print or save as a PDF.

Can I bring my old times along?

Yes, as a CSV file: Zeropunch’s own export or a simple list of date and time, for example saved from a spreadsheet. The import is in nerd mode. The file is read on your phone and never sent anywhere. The punches are encrypted like all the others.

I work night shifts. Does that work?

Yes. Turn on night shifts in the settings and choose when your day changes, for example at 04:00. A shift over midnight stays one day.

Is it free? Can I run it myself?

Yes. Zeropunch is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0. For a copy of your own you need a small serverless backend with a SQL database. The README on GitHub explains the setup.
